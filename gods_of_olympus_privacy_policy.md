# Privacy Policy for Gods of Olympus

**Effective Date:** September 19, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 19, 2026  

Welcome to **Gods of Olympus** ("the Game"), developed and maintained by **Tshiamo Jantjie** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy while delivering an immersive mythological idle RPG gaming experience.

---

### 1. Game Overview & Local Storage Architecture

Gods of Olympus is an idle action RPG set in the realm of Greek mythology. All game mechanics—including character stats, boss encounters, inventory items, equipment enhancements, offline idle rewards, and prestige levels—are stored and processed locally on your device via client-side storage ( / ).

We do not operate remote databases that harvest, aggregate, or monetize your gameplay data.

---

### 2. Information We Do Not Collect

- **No Mandatory Registration:** You can play, battle bosses, collect relics, and advance prestige tiers without creating an account or providing an email address or password.
- **No Financial Card Storage:** Any in-app purchases (such as gem bundles or battle passes) are securely processed through Google Play In-App Billing. We never receive or store your credit card or banking information.
- **No Device Contact or Media Scraping:** The Game never requests access to your address book, photos, camera, or location sensors.
- **No Commercial Data Brokerage:** We do not sell, trade, or share your gameplay statistics with third-party data brokers.

---

### 3. Optional Diagnostics & Telemetry

To ensure technical performance and crash resolution:
- **Crash Reports & Platform Telemetry:** Anonymous stack traces and device model information may be collected through standard Google Play Services to debug errors and maintain compatibility across Android devices.
- **Optional Ad Services:** If non-intrusive rewarded advertisements or analytics are active, standard pseudonymous advertising identifiers may be used in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.

---

### 4. Player Control & Complete Data Deletion

Because all game data resides on your physical device:
- **Data Erasure:** You can erase all saved characters, unlocked items, and progression at any time by clearing the Game's cache and data in your device settings (), or by uninstalling the Game.
- **No Remote Retention:** Deleting local data removes all gameplay history permanently.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy

Gods of Olympus is designed for general audiences and mythological RPG enthusiasts. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 years of age.

---

### 6. Policy Updates

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the Game constitutes acceptance of any posted changes.
